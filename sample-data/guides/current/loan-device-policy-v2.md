# Loan device policy

## Eligibility and stock
Check the current support agreement before offering a loan laptop. Confirm stock with the Service Desk or duty technician before promising a unit. An inventory snapshot shows the last recorded status.

## Pickup and return
The current pickup point is **Office B**. Standard pickup hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00–16:00 Europe/Helsinki time. The duty technician can confirm a separate handover arrangement where the agreement permits it. Record the loan unit ID, issue time and agreed return date in the ticket.

## After hours
Follow the [after-hours procedure](after-hours-support.md).
