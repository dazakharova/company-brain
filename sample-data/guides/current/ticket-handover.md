# Ticket priority and handover

## Record the impact
Record the number of affected users, the work that is blocked and whether an alternative device is available. Use the customer agreement and the [after-hours procedure](after-hours-support.md) to choose the next owner.

## Close the handover
Record actions already taken, the next action, the responsible team and the expected follow-up time. Distinguish a confirmed booking from an availability check.
