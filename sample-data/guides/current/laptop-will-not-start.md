# Laptop will not start

## Check power
Ask whether lights or a startup screen appear. If equipment is undamaged and safe to handle, disconnect the dock and external accessories. Try the approved charger directly in a known working power outlet.

## Choose the next step
If the laptop still shows no signs of power, record the checks and open a hardware repair request. Check the customer agreement and follow the [loan device policy](loan-device-policy-v2.md) if a loan may be needed.
