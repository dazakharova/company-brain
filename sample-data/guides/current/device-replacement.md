# Device replacement checklist

## Before the exchange
Verify the customer's authorisation and check the old and replacement device IDs. Confirm that the approved setup and required applications are ready.

## At handover
Ask the user to confirm that they can sign in and open a work application. Record the exchange and update the asset status. If this is a temporary device, follow the [loan device policy](loan-device-policy-v2.md).
