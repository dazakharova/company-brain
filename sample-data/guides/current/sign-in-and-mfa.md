# Sign-in and MFA problems

## Identify the issue
Record the affected service and the exact error message. Check whether the user can access other services and whether their authentication method has changed.

## Route the case
Follow the organisation's identity verification procedure before requesting changes. Route verified account recovery requests to Account Support and record the outcome in the ticket.
